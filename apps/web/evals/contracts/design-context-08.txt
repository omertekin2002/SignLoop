FREELANCE DESIGN AGREEMENT

This Freelance Design Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on October 5, 2026 between Quillfeather Design Studio ("Designer") and Brambleberry Tea Company Ltd. ("Client").

1. Project. Designer will create a packaging design system for Client's spring tea range, including three (3) initial concepts, two (2) rounds of revisions, and final print-ready files (the "Deliverables").

2. Schedule. Designer will deliver the initial concepts by October 26, 2026 and the final files by December 7, 2026, provided that Client gives consolidated feedback within five (5) business days of each delivery.

3. Fees. The project fee is $6,400.00. Client shall pay 40% on signing and 60% on delivery of the final files. Additional revision rounds are charged at $85.00 per hour.

4. Termination. Client may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving Designer 14 days' written notice. If Client terminates before delivery of the final files, Client shall pay a kill fee equal to fifty percent (50%) of the unpaid balance of the project fee, in addition to fees for work already completed.

5. Intellectual property. Designer assigns copyright in the final Deliverables to Client upon receipt of full payment. Designer keeps ownership of unused concepts and may display the Deliverables in its portfolio after Client's product launch.

6. Credit. Client will credit Designer on its website as "Packaging design: Quillfeather Design Studio".

7. Contact. Designer: studio@example.com. Client: marketing@example.org.

8. Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
