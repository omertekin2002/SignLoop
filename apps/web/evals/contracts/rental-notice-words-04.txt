EQUIPMENT RENTAL AGREEMENT

Owner: Copperleaf Equipment Rentals Ltd. ("Owner")
Renter: Juniper Street Studio LLC ("Renter")
Equipment: two (2) commercial espresso machines, serial numbers CE-2041 and CE-2042, with matching grinders (the "Equipment")
Delivery date: June 1, 2026
Owner contact: rentals@example.net, (720) 555-0186

1. Rental period. The initial rental period is six (6) months starting on the delivery date. After the initial rental period, this Agreement continues automatically from month to month until terminated under Section 6.

2. Rent. Renter shall pay rent of $310.00 per month, in advance, on the first day of each month. Rent is fixed for the initial rental period.

3. Security deposit. Renter shall pay a refundable security deposit of $500.00 before delivery. Owner will return the deposit, less the cost of repairing any damage beyond ordinary wear and tear, within fourteen (14) days after collecting the Equipment.

4. Care and use. Renter will keep the Equipment at Renter's premises, follow the manufacturer's cleaning instructions, and allow Owner to inspect the Equipment on reasonable notice.

5. Damage and loss. Renter is responsible for loss of or damage to the Equipment while it is in Renter's possession, other than ordinary wear and tear.

6. Termination. After the initial rental period, either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other party not less than thirty (30) days’ prior written notice. Renter must make the Equipment available for collection on the termination date.

7. Early return. If Renter returns the Equipment before the end of the initial rental period, Renter shall pay the rent for the remainder of the initial rental period.

8. Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Colorado.
