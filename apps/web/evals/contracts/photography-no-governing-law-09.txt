EVENT PHOTOGRAPHY AGREEMENT

This Event Photography Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on August 3, 2026 between Bright Fern Studio ("Photographer") and Quill & Compass Bookshop ("Client").

1. Services. Photographer will photograph Client's tenth-anniversary reading event on September 19, 2026 for up to four (4) hours and deliver at least 150 edited digital images.

2. Fee. Client will pay a flat fee of $1,800.00. A deposit of $450.00 is due when Client signs this Agreement, and the balance is due on the event date.

3. Delivery. Photographer will deliver the edited images through a private online gallery within twenty-one (21) days after the event.

4. Cancellation. Client may cancel this Agreement by giving Photographer 30 calendar days' written notice before the event date, and Photographer will refund the deposit. If Client cancels with less notice, the deposit is non-refundable.

5. Term. This Agreement ends when Photographer has delivered the images and Client has paid the balance. It does not renew.

6. Usage. Client may use the images for its own marketing. Photographer may show up to ten (10) of the images in its portfolio.

7. Liability. Photographer's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fee Client has paid.

8. Notices. Notices must be sent by email to Photographer at studio@example.com and to Client at events@example.org.
