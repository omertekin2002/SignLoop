DESIGN RETAINER AGREEMENT (2025)

Between Quillfeather Design Studio ("Designer") and Brambleberry Tea Company Ltd. ("Client").

1. Term. This retainer runs from January 6, 2025 to December 31, 2025.

2. Renewal. This retainer renews automatically for successive one (1) year terms unless either party gives sixty (60) days' written notice of non-renewal.

3. Retainer fee. Client pays a monthly retainer of $1,800.00 for up to twenty (20) hours of design work. Unused hours do not carry forward.

4. Disputes. Any dispute arising out of this retainer shall be resolved by binding arbitration in London.

5. Ownership. Designer retains copyright in all work until the end of the retainer year.
