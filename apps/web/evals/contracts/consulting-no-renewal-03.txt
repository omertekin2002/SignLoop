CONSULTING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Consulting Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on May 4, 2026 between Marigold Lane Consulting LLC ("Consultant") and Teal Harbor Community Alliance ("Client").

1. Services. Consultant will deliver two grant-writing workshops for Client's staff and review up to four (4) grant applications, as described in Exhibit A.

2. Term. This Agreement begins on May 11, 2026 and ends on November 30, 2026. This Agreement does not renew automatically; any extension must be agreed in a written amendment signed by both parties.

3. Fees. Client will pay Consultant $95.00 per hour, invoiced monthly in arrears, up to a maximum of $12,000.00 for the term unless Client approves additional hours in writing. Invoices are due within 30 days of receipt.

4. Expenses. Client will reimburse reasonable travel expenses that Client approves in advance.

5. Termination for convenience. Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason by giving the other party 15 calendar days' written notice. Client will pay for services performed through the termination date, and no other termination fee applies.

6. Confidentiality. Each party will keep the other party's non-public information confidential and use it only for this Agreement.

7. Ownership. Client owns the grant applications and workshop materials prepared specifically for Client. Consultant keeps its pre-existing templates and general know-how.

8. Liability. Each party's liability under this Agreement is limited to the total fees paid or payable under this Agreement, except for breaches of Section 6.

9. Independent contractor. Consultant is an independent contractor and is responsible for its own taxes and insurance.

10. Notices. Notices must be sent by email to Consultant at hello@example.net and to Client at office@example.org.

11. Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Oregon.

Exhibit A - Scope
- Workshop 1: Finding and qualifying grant opportunities (June 2026)
- Workshop 2: Writing persuasive program narratives (September 2026)
- Application reviews: up to four (4) applications, each returned within five (5) business days
