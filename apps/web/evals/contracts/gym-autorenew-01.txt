1. TERM
1.1 The initial term of this Agreement begins on March 1, 2026 (the “Start Date”) and ends on February 28, 2027 (the “Initial Term”).
1.2 Automatic renewal. Unless cancelled under Section 4, this Agreement renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month terms (each a “Renewal Term”). The first Renewal Term begins on March 1, 2027 (the “Renewal Date”), and each later Renewal Term begins on the anniversary of that date.

2. DUES AND FEES
2.1 Monthly dues of $49.00 are charged on the first day of each month to the payment method on file.
2.2 An annual facility maintenance fee of $59.00 is charged on the Start Date and on each anniversary of the Start Date.
2.3 A late fee of $15.00 applies to any payment that remains unpaid ten (10) days after its due date.
2.4 The Club may change the monthly dues for any Renewal Term by posting the new rate at the front desk at least thirty (30) days before that Renewal Term begins.

3. USE OF THE CLUB
3.1 The Member may use all Club facilities during staffed hours, subject to the posted Club rules.
3.2 Guests must sign a guest waiver and are limited to four (4) visits per calendar year.
3.3 The Club may close areas of the facility for maintenance, holidays, or events without a reduction in dues.

4. CANCELLATION
4.1 To cancel at the end of the Initial Term or any Renewal Term, the Member must deliver written notice to the Club at least ten (10) business days before the Renewal Date. Notice may be delivered in person at the front desk or by email to cancellations@example.com, and the Club will confirm receipt in writing.
4.2 Early cancellation. If the Member cancels during the Initial Term for any reason other than relocation of more than twenty-five (25) miles or a documented medical inability to exercise, the Member shall pay an early termination fee equal to fifty percent (50%) of the monthly dues remaining in the Initial Term.
4.3 Membership freezes of up to three (3) months per year are available for a fee of $10.00 per month and do not extend the Initial Term.

5. LIABILITY
5.1 The Member uses the facilities at the Member's own risk. The Club is not responsible for lost or stolen personal property.

6. GENERAL
6.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois. Any dispute will be heard in the state or federal courts located in Cook County, Illinois.
6.2 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Club and the Member about the membership.
